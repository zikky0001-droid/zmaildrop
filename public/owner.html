01 / ABOUT

The human behind the mailbox.

🤞 MY BIO

Tech enthusiast, builder, problem-solver.

I'm a full-stack developer who loves turning ideas into working products — often from my phone, in Termux, at odd hours. My range runs from AI chat assistants and disposable email infrastructure to manga readers, Telegram and WhatsApp bots, and cybersecurity awareness tools. If it's useful, weird, or fun to build, I've probably already started it.

Most of what I build starts the same way: I notice something annoying or missing, and instead of waiting for someone else to fix it, I open a terminal and start typing. That's how ZMAIL Drop happened — people constantly need a quick, throwaway inbox for sign-ups and one-time codes, and most existing options are cluttered or push you toward paid plans. So I built a clean, fast, completely free one.

It's the same story behind ZIKKY AI — a multi-model assistant that routes between Gemini, DeepSeek and Meta AI so users get the best answer instead of being locked into one provider. And Manga Hub, a reader I built because the existing ones were slow and ad-infested. Each project taught me something new: API auth, session lifecycles, rate limits, deployment pipelines, the difference between "it works on my machine" and "it works for everyone."

I don't claim to know everything. I claim to be willing to figure it out — one error message, one Stack Overflow thread, one late-night debugging session at a time.
