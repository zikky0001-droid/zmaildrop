03 / FAQ

Good to know before you use it.

Are these inboxes private?+

No. Treat a temporary mailbox as public. Someone who knows the address may be able to access the same inbox.

Do I get a password?+

No password is assigned. This is part of what makes the mailbox quick to open, but it also means it should not be used for sensitive information.

How long does an inbox last?+

Temporary storage is not permanent. Inboxes can be cleared when they become inactive, including when an inbox does not receive a message within the service's inactivity window. Do not rely on it as long-term storage.

Can I send email from here?+

ZMAIL Drop is designed around receiving and reading temporary messages. It is not intended to be a normal personal email account.
